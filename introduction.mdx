## Your role decides what you see

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="User" icon="user">
    Search customers, view credit, analyse statements. Read-only on the bureau dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="user-shield">
    Everything a User can do, plus export data and download Excel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Super Admin" icon="user-tie">
    Full access — invite people, change roles, manage fields, configure the app.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If a button or menu item is missing for you, your role doesn't include it. Ask a Super Admin.
