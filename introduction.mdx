This guide is for everyone on the Capinex sales team. It walks you through every screen of the Capinex Finance app and shows you how to do your day-to-day work, one tap at a time.

## What is Capinex Finance?

It's the app you use every day to look up customers, check their credit, run loan applications, and read bank statements. You sign in with your work email, and what you can do depends on your role.

## Your role decides what you see

There are three roles in Capinex Finance:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="User" icon="user">
    You can search customers, view credit, and analyse statements. You can't change settings or invite people.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="user-shield">
    Everything a User can do, plus you can export data and download reports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Super Admin" icon="user-tie">
    Full access. You can also invite new people, change roles, manage fields, and configure the app.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If a button or menu item is missing for you, it's because of your role. Ask a Super Admin if you think you need access.
